About Mera Flow

Protocolized liquidity for the Mera X ecosystem

Mera Flow is the protocolized flow module within the Mera X ecosystem, designed to support digital asset circulation, ecosystem access, and transparent participation.

Users can connect a decentralized wallet, select supported stablecoin assets such as USDT or USDC, and participate in Mera Flow based on the circulation periods displayed on the page. Every participation record is executed by smart contracts and settled on-chain according to the corresponding period.

The core goal of Mera Flow is to build a more transparent, automated, and open Web3 liquidity participation mechanism.

How your return is calculated

The maturity settlement amount is derived directly from the protocol display rate applied to your flow amount.

Flow Amount×Protocol Rate=Maturity Settlement Amount

For example, locking 10,000 USDT / USDC in the 28-day flow:

10,000×124%=12,400 USDT / USDC

Maturity settlement

Once a Mera Flow order is created, the system generates a corresponding on-chain record based on the flow period you selected. When the period ends, the smart contract executes settlement according to protocol rules and returns the corresponding assets to your connected wallet address.

Settlement is executed by smart contract after the selected flow period ends.

Risk disclosure

Mera Flow is a Web3 liquidity participation module based on smart contracts. Before participating, users should fully understand the risks associated with smart contracts, on-chain assets, market volatility, network congestion, and protocol execution.

The settlement data displayed on the page is calculated based on current protocol parameters only, and the actual execution results shall be subject to the on-chain smart contract records.

Partner Rewards

Earn from your ecosystem network

Mera Flow supports an ecosystem partner reward mechanism. When users participate through a compliant invitation relationship and form valid on-chain circulation records, eligible ecosystem partners can receive corresponding community contribution rewards based on protocol rules.

Activation Rules

The amount a user personally circulates in Mera Flow determines the partner reward tiers they can unlock. Different circulation amounts correspond to different partner reward ranges.

Partner reward formula

Partner Reward=Referred Flow Reward×Partner Reward Rate

Example: If a referred user participates with 10,000 USDT, selects the 1 Day flow period, and earns a flow reward of 40 USDT, a Tier 1 partner with a 20% reward rate would receive:

40×20%=8 USDT
